# Clinical Protocol: Blood Test Preparation

## Protocol ID
LAB-PREP-001

## Effective Date
2026-01-15

## Applicability
Applies to all adult patients (18+) scheduled for routine or diagnostic blood collection at LDP Labs Clinic.

---

## Pre-Collection Instructions

### Fasting Requirements
- **Standard fasting**: 8–12 hours of absolute fasting is required.
- Water intake is permitted during the fasting period.
- No chewing gum, hard candy, or flavored beverages during fasting.

### Dietary Restrictions (24 hours before collection)
- No alcoholic beverages.
- No fatty or fried foods.
- Avoid caffeine (coffee, tea, energy drinks).
- Maintain usual hydration with water only.

### Medication Guidance
- Patients should take their regular prescribed medications as usual, unless explicitly contraindicated by their physician.
- Inform the phlebotomist of all medications taken within the last 24 hours.

### Activity Restrictions
- Avoid intense physical exercise for 12 hours prior to collection.
- Remain seated and relaxed for at least 15 minutes before blood draw.

---

## Special Instructions by Test Type

| Test                       | Fasting Required | Additional Notes                           |
|----------------------------|------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| Fasting Glucose            | Yes (8–12h)      | Water only                                  |
| Lipid Panel                | Yes (12h)        | No alcohol for 24h                          |
| HbA1c                      | No               | No special preparation required             |
| Complete Blood Count (CBC) | No               | No special preparation required             |
| TSH / Thyroid Panel        | No               | Inform if taking biotin supplements         |
| Vitamin D                  | No               | No special preparation required             |
| Iron / Ferritin            | Yes (12h)        | Morning collection preferred                |
| Liver Function (ALT/AST)   | No               | Avoid alcohol for 24h                       |

---

## Post-Collection Care
- Apply firm pressure to the puncture site for 2–3 minutes.
- Keep the bandage on for at least 4 hours.
- Avoid heavy lifting or strenuous activity with the arm used for collection for 2 hours.
- If bruising occurs, apply a cold compress in intervals of 15 minutes.

---

## Contraindications and Warnings
- Do NOT collect from the arm on the side of a mastectomy, AV fistula, or lymph node dissection without physician approval.
- Patients with a known bleeding disorder or on anticoagulant therapy must inform the phlebotomist before collection.
